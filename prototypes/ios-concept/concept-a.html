Concept A · night sky

Home is the sidebar's sky stretched to a phone: every firefly on every machine, by who needs you. Spaces, panes and mods open as sheets stacked over the sky; swipe down to go back. One axis of travel: deeper or back to the sky.

gains: the agents are the front door: a blocked agent is one tap from its terminal, wherever it runs; it reads as nocturne at first glance. costs: spaces and mods sit one sheet down (a grabber), so a "just browse my machine" visit is a step longer; deep stacks need the swipe-down habit.
